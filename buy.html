how to get duk.

five steps. no charts.
if anything looks off, stop. lying down is always an option.

the steps

  1. 1

    get a wallet.

    duk lives on solana. phantom and solflare both work: the app on your phone or the extension in your browser.

    phantom solflare

    write the recovery phrase on paper. nobody ever needs it: not us, not support, not a website. anyone who asks for it is robbing you.

  2. 2

    put some sol in it.

    you swap sol for duk. buy sol inside the wallet, or on an exchange and send it to your wallet's address.

    leave a little sol in there for network fees.

  3. 3

    copy the real ca.

    this is the only one. it ends in pump.

    DG2EJSEtPBge5F7zBCTZG78p27LTCiq8tUkJ39qupump

    check it matches pump.fun and the pinned post on @justdukfun. ignore addresses in replies and dms.

  4. 4

    swap sol for duk.

    open one of these with duk already filled in, connect your wallet, pick how much sol, check the token says duk with the address above, then confirm in the wallet.

    jupiter pump.fun

    on your phone? open jupiter inside the wallet app:
    in phantom / in solflare

  5. 5

    then duk.

    it's in your wallet. that's the whole thing. now the fun part:

    • make a duk
    • learn the position
    • steal some memes
